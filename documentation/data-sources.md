# Data Sources and Provenance Document
## Project 628 Aerospace — Microgravity Fire Intelligence (Mark 1)

### 1. Data Integrity & Provenance Standards
In accordance with aerospace research software requirements:
- **No Artificial Inflation**: Statistics display the exact count of verified flight investigations (8 core detailed records) and primary literature papers (8 NASA Technical Memorandums / journal publications) indexed in Mark 1.
- **Handling of Missing Values**: Missing parameters are explicitly rendered as `"Not available in source data"` rather than estimated or synthetically populated.
- **Traceability**: Every experiment record links directly to its Principal Investigator, lead research center, and NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS) accession number or DOI.

### 2. Primary Flight & Ground Research Programmes Indexed

#### Saffire (Spacecraft Fire Safety Demonstration)
- **Facility**: Autonomous Cygnus cargo resupply spacecraft (post-ISS unberthing)
- **Lead Center**: NASA Glenn Research Center (GRC)
- **Principal Investigators**: David L. Urban, Ph.D., Gary A. Ruff, Ph.D.
- **Investigated Fuels**: SIBAL fabric (75% cotton / 25% fiberglass), PMMA cast sheets, Nomex.
- **Key Contributions**: First large-scale flame spread experiments in microgravity; validation of low-flow upward flame spread; exploration atmosphere flammability (34% O2, 56.5 kPa / 8.2 psia).
- **NTRS References**: NASA/TM-20205003290, NASA/TM-20230001928.

#### BASS & BASS-II (Burning and Suppression of Solids)
- **Facility**: ISS Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG) / Combustion Integrated Rack (CIR)
- **Lead Center**: NASA Glenn Research Center & Case Western Reserve University
- **Principal Investigators**: Sandra L. Olson, Ph.D., James S. T'ien, Ph.D.
- **Investigated Fuels**: PMMA cylindrical rods (6.35 mm & 12.7 mm diameter) and flat slabs.
- **Key Contributions**: Mapping the non-monotonic flammability U-curve; identification of low-speed (1–5 cm/s) airflow hazard regime; nitrogen gaseous suppression limits.
- **NTRS References**: NASA/TM-2016-218990.

#### FLEX & FLEX-2 (Flame Extinction Experiment)
- **Facility**: ISS CIR Multi-User Droplet Combustion Apparatus (MDCA)
- **Lead Center**: NASA Glenn Research Center & UC San Diego
- **Principal Investigators**: Forman A. Williams, Ph.D., Vedha Nayagam, Ph.D.
- **Investigated Fuels**: Isolated n-heptane, n-octane, and methanol droplets (1.5–4.0 mm).
- **Key Contributions**: Discovery of quasi-steady cool flames burning invisibly at 600–900 K following radiative hot flame extinction; measurement of droplet evaporation constants ($d^2$ law).
- **NTRS References**: NASA/TM-20140010480.

#### ACME (Advanced Combustion via Microgravity Experiments)
- **Facility**: ISS CIR Chamber
- **Sub-investigations**: Coflow Flame Extinction (CFI), Burning Rate Emulator (BRE), s-Flame.
- **Lead Centers**: University of Maryland, Case Western Reserve University, NASA GRC
- **Investigated Fuels**: Gaseous methane (CH4), ethylene (C2H4).
- **Key Contributions**: Isolating chemical kinetic extinction from convective strain; CO2 vs N2 suppression efficacy; radiative emission from soot aggregates.
- **NTRS References**: NASA/TM-20190033281.

#### SOFIE (Solid Fuel Ignition and Extinction)
- **Facility**: ISS CIR Chamber
- **Lead Center**: UC Berkeley & NASA Glenn Research Center
- **Investigated Fuels**: PMMA, polycarbonate, Nomex under external radiant thermal flux.
- **Key Contributions**: Establishing that radiant heat from warm avionics drastically lowers low-flow extinction thresholds.
- **NTRS References**: NASA/TM-20220004128.

#### MGM (Microgravity Smoldering Combustion) & MIST (Water Mist)
- **Facilities**: Space Shuttle STS-107, NASA Glenn 2.2-Second Drop Tower
- **Key Contributions**: Persistence of flameless smoldering in polyurethane foam down to 12.5% O2; abrupt transition to flaming upon ventilation restoration; ultra-fine water mist (20–45 μm) quenching microgravity diffusion flames with 70% less agent mass.
- **NTRS References**: NASA/TM-20210013994, NASA/TM-20180007812.
